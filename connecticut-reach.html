What we handle

Commercial brokerage and advisory for the decisions that matter.

Matthews keeps the work close to the broker. The person advising you can talk through the building, the likely buyer or tenant pool, comparable trades, and what is realistic in the market right now.

01

Investment sales

Sale and purchase representation for owner-users, private investors, families, and local operators. Typical assignments range from $250,000 to $4,000,000, with larger opportunities handled by relationship.

  • Off-market sourcing through repeat-client network
  • Income, comp, and replacement-cost framing
  • Owner-user vs. investor positioning
02

Leasing

Industrial, flex, medical, professional office, retail, and restaurant leasing from 1,000 SF suites to larger 50,000 SF requirements.

  • Speculative and build-to-suit positioning
  • Subdivision strategies for large vacant boxes
  • Tenant credit and use review
03

Tenant & landlord representation

Expansions, relocations, renewals, vacancy strategy, and landlord representation. Many assignments start with a product-knowledge problem: what actually exists, who is active, and what terms are realistic.

  • Requirement definition and short-listing
  • Concessions, TI, and term negotiation
  • Lease audit and renewal strategy
04

Valuation & advisory

Broker opinion of value grounded in comparable sales, income, lease terms in place, and tenant quality. Used by owners, lenders, attorneys, and estates that need a practical number before they make a move.

  • Comparable sales and lease research
  • Income approach with rent-roll stress tests
  • Pre-listing & estate / partnership valuations

Brokerage, leasing, valuation, and advisory stay at the center of the firm. Property management should only appear on the final site if the business confirms it is an active service.

About the firm

A small firm, on purpose.

Matthews Commercial Properties has been brokering commercial real estate in Connecticut for decades. The firm is small on purpose: direct broker involvement, practical advice, and an unusually high share of repeat clients.

Most assignments begin with a call from someone we already know: an owner considering a sale, a tenant looking for space, a landlord with vacancy, or a family that needs a practical value on a property. Because we know the buildings, owners, tenants, and prior trades in this market, we can move quickly and give direct advice.

Last year, the firm closed more than 76 transactions. The message should be simple: clients call Matthews because the brokers understand the property, the people, and the practical path forward.
