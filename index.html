What we handle

Four ways we help owners, tenants, and investors make better real estate decisions.

We are a small firm on purpose. Every assignment is handled by a licensed broker — not passed down to a salesperson — so the person on the call has context on the building, the block, and the comparable trades.

01

Sales & acquisitions

Sale and purchase representation for owner-users, private investors, and local families who own commercial property. We work in the $250,000 to $4,000,000 range as a matter of routine, with larger assignments by relationship.

  • Off-market sourcing through repeat-client network
  • Income, comp, and replacement-cost framing
  • Owner-user vs. investor positioning
02

Leasing

Suites from 1,000 SF up to 50,000 SF — industrial, flex, medical, professional office, retail, and restaurant. We currently run a heavy medical book on the landlord side.

  • Speculative and build-to-suit positioning
  • Subdivision strategies for large vacant boxes
  • Tenant credit and use review
03

Tenant & landlord representation

Expansions, relocations, renewals, and consolidations. Most assignments start with a product-knowledge problem: what actually exists in the submarket, and at what effective rate.

  • Requirement definition and short-listing
  • Concessions, TI, and term negotiation
  • Lease audit and renewal strategy
04

Valuation & advisory

Broker opinion of value grounded in comparable sales, income, lease terms in place, and tenant quality. Used by owners, lenders, attorneys, and estates that need a practical number before they make a move.

  • Comparable sales and lease research
  • Income approach with rent-roll stress tests
  • Pre-listing & estate / partnership valuations

We do not offer property management. That is intentional. It keeps our time and judgment focused on sales, leasing, tenant representation, and valuation.

About the firm

A small firm, on purpose.

Matthews Commercial Properties has been brokering commercial real estate in Western Connecticut for decades. The firm is small on purpose: three broker-licensed team members, a focused regional footprint, and an unusually high share of repeat clients.

Most assignments begin with a call from someone we already know: an owner considering a sale, a tenant looking for space, a landlord with vacancy, or a family that needs a practical value on a property. Because we know the buildings, owners, tenants, and prior trades in this market, we can move quickly and give direct advice.

Last year, the three of us closed more than 76 transactions. We do not run a sales floor. We do not hand assignments off. We do not manage property. We do this work.
